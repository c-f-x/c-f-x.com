Deploy once. Attach every product.

Run one copy of each tool, however many products you make. Each product connects through an address on its own domain and keeps its own data. The tools are built to fit inside Cloudflare’s Workers Paid plan, $5 a month, so at small-team size a new product adds no cost.

the tools

Seven tools on one shared kit

Each tool does one job. Run the one you need, or all of them: they share one kit, one look and one way to deploy.

  1. ARow A, watch

    what people do, and whether it works

    • cfx-analytics ↗product analytics Counts pages and events, records sessions and draws heatmaps, from PostHog’s own SDKs. Each product’s data is kept apart, in the region you choose. live
    • cfx-errorserror tracking Collects the errors your apps throw, and groups the ones that share a cause. next
    • cfx-statusuptime and status pages Checks that your sites answer and that your scheduled jobs still check in. A public status page shows the result. next

  2. BRow B, reach

    how your product talks to people

    • cfx-mail ↗mail for your domains Mailboxes and sending for your own domains. Your apps send their sign-in links and alerts through it. live
    • cfx-launchlaunch pages and signups Launch pages, waitlists and tracked links for a new product. It counts who visits and who signs up, from each link you share. next

  3. CRow C, keep

    what you can get back

    • cfx-backupbackups Copies your data off Cloudflare on a schedule. Each copy is restored in a test, so you know it works. later

  4. DRow D, run

    fixing your data by hand

    • cfx-adminadmin panels A panel to read and fix the data in any of your products, for you and for your agents. next

  5. the kit

    what every tool stands on

    • cfx-kit ↗the shared kit The parts every tool shares, such as health checks and rule checks. Its deploy runner shows the plan, and how to undo each step, before it acts. built
