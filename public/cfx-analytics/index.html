Analytics on your own account.

product analytics

Your sites and apps send events with PostHog’s own SDKs, unchanged. cfx-analytics keeps them on your Cloudflare account, records sessions, draws heatmaps and answers questions from one dashboard.

why

Your visitors’ data, on your own account.

Hosted analytics keeps your visitors’ data on someone else’s servers. cfx-analytics runs in your Cloudflare account instead, one deployment for every product you make. A product keeps the SDK it already uses and changes one line, the host it sends to. Each product’s data is kept apart, in the region you choose, for as long as you set.

what it answers

  • how many came, against the week before
  • which pages, sources, countries and devices
  • where people drop out of a funnel
  • who comes back, week after week
  • how far down a page people read
  • what a visit looked like, as a recording
  • where people click, and where they click in vain
  • what your own product’s server asks, with its own key

how

One line to connect, one place to read

  1. 1

    Point a product at its own address

    Each product sends to an address on its own domain, so a visitor’s browser sends to no one else’s. With posthog-js it is the host:

    posthog.init("<the project’s public token>", {
  api_host: "https://e.shop.example.com",
})
  2. 2

    Every product keeps its own store

    Events, people, recordings and heatmap points live in the product’s own store, apart from every other product’s. Known bots, AI crawlers and your own visits are marked and left out of every answer, unless you ask for them.

  3. 3

    Read every product on one board

    One dashboard holds every product. You sign in with your Cloudflare account, so there is no other password to keep.

  4. 4

    Let a product read its own numbers

    A product’s server asks with a secret key, in the same shape the dashboard uses:

    curl https://e.shop.example.com/api/external/query \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $CFANALYTICS_SECRET_KEY" \
  -d '{"kind": "trend", "measure": "visitors",
       "granularity": "day",
       "range": {"kind": "last_days", "days": 7}}'
  5. 5

    Keep only what you mean to keep

    Events, recordings and heatmap points each have a retention period, and a daily sweep removes what is older. A person can be erased on request, and a product’s data exported.

what it doesn’t do

  • flags and experimentsNo feature flags, surveys or experiments.
  • errorsNo error tracking. That is cfx-errors’ job, next in the family.
  • PostHog’s query languageQuestions are asked as JSON, in the shape the dashboard uses.
  • more peopleThe dashboard has one user, the owner of the Cloudflare account.

install

Install

You need a Cloudflare account on the Workers Paid plan, which starts at $5 a month, a domain on Cloudflare for the dashboard, and Node 24. Get the code, make the stores and the sign-in client once, write your values into a private site file, then deploy. Every command prints its plan and how to undo each step, and changes nothing until you add --go:

git clone https://github.com/c-f-x/cfx-analytics
cd cfx-analytics
pnpm install
pnpm ops secrets production --go
pnpm ops deploy production --go
pnpm ops watch --go
pnpm smoke production

Every step is in the README.
